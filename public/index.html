What we build

Three builds. One standard.

Every job is measured and framed on-site — nothing pre-fabricated, nothing forced to fit.

01 / Deck

Decks

Raised or ground-level decking that extends the house outward — built around downpipes, slopes and existing trees rather than against them.

Typical timberMerbau / Spotted Gum
Best forSloped blocks, entertaining
02 / Patio

Patios

Covered, all-weather patios that give you a usable outdoor room through Gippsland's wetter months, not just the summer ones.

Typical roofingInsulated / polycarb
Best forYear-round living space
03 / Pergola

Pergolas

Open-frame structures for shade and definition — a lighter-touch build for framing a spa, alfresco area or garden entertaining space.

Typical timberTreated pine / hardwood
Best forShade, garden zoning
How a build runs

From first measure to handover.

The same five stages on every job, so you always know what's next.

01

Consult & measure

We walk the site with you, talk through how the space will actually be used, and take accurate measurements.

02

Design & council check

Layout, timber and finish are confirmed, and we handle any Baw Baw Shire permit requirements before work starts.

03

Footings & frame

Footings are set to depth for our local ground conditions, then the frame goes up square and level.

04

Decking, screening & finish

Boards, balustrades or screening are fitted, then oiled or stained ready for weather.

05

Walkthrough & handover

We walk the finished build with you, cover care and maintenance, and hand it over.
