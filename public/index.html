How a build runs

From first measure to handover.

The same five stages on every job, so you always know what's next.

01

Consult & measure

We visit your home, talk through how you will use the space and take accurate measurements.

02

Design & quote

We confirm the design, materials and finish, give you a clear quote, and help with any council permit requirements.

03

Prepare & order

Site preparation, ordering materials and made-to-measure items so the install runs smoothly.

04

Build & install

Our team builds, fits and finishes the work to a professional standard.

05

Walkthrough & handover

We walk through the finished job with you, cover care and maintenance, and hand it over.
